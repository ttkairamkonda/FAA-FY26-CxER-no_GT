Catching ATC Errors Without a Reference Transcript

The demo above showed CxER judging an ASR transcription against a known-correct reference. Here there's no reference at all — these are full pilot/controller conversations reconstructed by an LLM from real ASRS incident reports, and the system has to decide on its own whether the communication was correct.
